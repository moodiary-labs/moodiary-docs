# Categories, search and calendar

Use categories, search, and the calendar to revisit your entries.

## Categories

Create and manage categories under **Me → Categories**, and assign an entry to one when writing. The home list can be filtered by category.

## Full-text search

The search box at the top of the home screen uses a **SQLite FTS5** full-text index:

- It handles Chinese, English, and mixed input;
- Results are ranked by relevance.

## Semantic search

Use **semantic search** to search by meaning when you do not remember the exact keywords:

- Download and select an embedding model under **Settings → Services → Semantic search**; download size depends on the model;
- After that you can search with a natural-language description, and results are ranked by semantic relevance.

The model and its index are also stored locally — nothing is uploaded.

## Calendar view

Open **Me → Revisit → Calendar** to browse by month or year and filter by category. Tap a day to read its entries. The Chinese calendar also shows lunar dates, solar terms, and festivals.

## Mood insights

Choose one of five mood levels when writing, or leave it blank. Open **Me → Mood insights** to review your mood calendar, trends, and distribution by week, month, or year.

Upgrading to 2.9.0 converts earlier mood values into levels. Scene values such as Study and Travel become tags.

## Media library

Every image, audio clip, and video you've ever inserted shows up in the **Media library**, where you can browse by type or jump straight to the entry that uses it.

## Trash

Deleted entries go to **Me → Trash**, where you can restore them or delete them permanently. The trash can also be filtered by category.
